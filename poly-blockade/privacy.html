Poly Blockade privacy policy

Effective October 14, 2026

This policy explains what information Poly Blockade collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. Poly Blockade is published by Liminal Games LLC ("Liminal Games Studio", "we", "us").

  • We collect what we need to run the game: your player ID, your saved progress, and analytics and crash data.
  • Poly Blockade has no ads. We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it for targeted advertising.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time.

Information we collect

Player ID

Poly Blockade doesn't have its own accounts or passwords. The game creates a random player ID on your device. You can see it at the bottom of the Settings menu and give it to us when you ask for support. We use it to find the data linked to your copy of the game. Signing in with Game Center on iOS or Google Play Games on Android is handled by Apple or Google, and we don't receive your password or display name.

Saved progress

Your progress and settings are saved to iCloud on iOS and Google Play Games on Android so you can continue on another device. It stays with Apple or Google, under your iCloud or Google Play Games account, and we can't read it.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We never receive your payment card details or your name. The game asks the store what you own so it can deliver your purchases and restore them with Restore Purchases. Apple and Google also give us sales reports that don't identify you.

Analytics and crash data

We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google, to understand how the game is played and to fix bugs. These services collect:

  • Device model, operating system version, language and game version
  • Approximate country or region, based on your IP address
  • Gameplay events, such as levels started and completed, and how long you play
  • Crash logs and error reports
  • An identifier for your copy of the game

We don't use your device's advertising identifier.

Messages you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

How we use your information

  • To run the game, sign you in and sync your progress across devices
  • To deliver and restore your purchases
  • To find and fix crashes and bugs
  • To understand how the game is played so we can improve it
  • To answer your support requests
  • To meet our legal obligations

Who we share it with

  • Apple and Google, which run the app stores, process purchases, and provide sign-in and cloud saves through Game Center, iCloud and Google Play Games. Their use of your information is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Service providers that run parts of the game for us: Google (Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics, see also Firebase privacy and security). They may use your information only to provide their service to us.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.
  • A buyer or successor, if our business is sold or merged. This policy would continue to apply to your information.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

How long we keep it

  • Player ID and saved progress: saved progress stays on your device and in your iCloud or Google Play Games storage until you delete it with Delete Account in the game. When you ask us to delete your data, we delete what we hold within 30 days.
  • Analytics and crash data: up to 14 months.
  • Support emails: for as long as we need them to help you, and then we delete them.
  • Purchase records: as long as the law requires us to keep them for tax and accounting.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time. The support page explains how.

You can also ask us what information we hold about you, ask for a copy of it, or ask us to correct it, by emailing contact@liminalgamesstudio.com. We'll answer within 30 days and won't treat you differently for asking.

United States

Depending on your state, for example California, Colorado or Virginia, you may have the right to know what personal information we collect, get a copy of it, correct it and delete it. We don't sell or share personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of.

European Economic Area and United Kingdom

We process your information to provide the game you asked for, to keep it working and improve it (our legitimate interests), and to meet legal obligations. You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to our processing of your information, and to receive it in a portable format. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

We're based in the United States. Our service providers may process your information in the United States and other countries.

Children

Poly Blockade is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Security

We rely on the security of our service providers and limit access to your information to the people who need it to run the game. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we'll also tell you in the game.

Contact

For questions about this policy or your information, email contact@liminalgamesstudio.com.
