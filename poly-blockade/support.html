Common questions

How do I restore my purchases?

On iPhone and iPad, open [menu path, e.g. Settings, then Restore Purchases] in the game. On Android, your purchases come back automatically when you're signed in to the same Google account you bought them with.

How do I move my progress to a new device?

Sign in on the new device with the same [account type, e.g. Game Center or Google Play Games account] you used before. Your saved progress loads automatically.

Can I get a refund?

Apple and Google handle all payments, so refunds go through them. On iPhone and iPad, request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, follow Google Play's refund instructions.

Delete your account and data

You can ask us to delete your Poly Blockade account and the data linked to it at any time.

To ask by email:

  • Email contact@liminalgamesstudio.com with "Delete my Poly Blockade data" in the subject line.
  • Include your player ID so we can find your account.
  • We'll confirm by email and delete your data within 30 days.

What we delete: your account, your saved progress, and the analytics data linked to your player ID.

What we keep: records of your purchases, for as long as the law requires for tax and accounting. Apple and Google also keep their own purchase records.

Scores and achievements in Game Center and Google Play Games are stored by Apple and Google. You can manage or remove them in those services' settings.
