Poly Blockade support

Get help

Email contact@liminalgamesstudio.com with "Poly Blockade support" in the subject line. To help us fix the problem quickly, include:

  • Your device model and its iOS or Android version
  • The game version, shown at the bottom of the Settings menu
  • Your player ID, shown at the bottom of the Settings menu, under the version. Tap it to copy it
  • What happened and what you expected, with screenshots if you can

Common questions

How do I restore my purchases?

Purchases belong to your Apple or Google account. On a new device or after reinstalling, open Settings and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the same account you bought with. Heroes and towers you bought come back. Gem packs are used up when you buy them, so they can't be restored.

If a purchase is missing after you've signed in, email us (see Get help above) and include your player ID.

How do I move my progress to a new device?

Your progress is saved to the cloud as you play. On iPhone and iPad it uses iCloud, so sign in to the same Apple ID with iCloud turned on. On Android it uses Google Play Games, so sign in to the same Google account. Your saved progress then loads automatically.

Can I get a refund?

Apple and Google handle all payments, so refunds go through them. On iPhone and iPad, request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, follow Google Play's refund instructions.

Delete your account and data

You can ask us to delete your Poly Blockade account and the data linked to it at any time.

The quickest way is in the game: open Settings and tap Delete Account. Tap it a second time within a few seconds to confirm. This erases all of your save slots on the device and in the cloud, and it can't be undone.

If you can't open the game, ask by email:

  • Email contact@liminalgamesstudio.com with "Delete my Poly Blockade data" in the subject line.
  • We'll confirm by email and delete your data within 30 days.

Apple and Google keep their own purchase records.
