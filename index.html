Leave No Witnesses

A first-person stealth-action game for PC. Get in, find your target and get out. Guards hunt you by sight and sound, civilians run for help, and anything you leave behind can give you away.

  • Light and sound decide

    Pools of darkness hide you. Footsteps carry. Crouch behind the bar and the guard walks past.

  • You see it coming

    Losing stealth is never a surprise. An edge-of-screen indicator fills toward whoever is noticing you.

  • Consequences stay

    A wounded guard bleeds and screams. Blood trails get followed, and bodies left in the open get found.
