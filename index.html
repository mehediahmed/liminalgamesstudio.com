Games

Poly Blockade

A low-poly tower defense game for iPhone, iPad and Android. It's coming soon, and it has no ads.

Creeps walk the road to your castle gate. You build towers on the pads beside it, and where you put them decides who reaches the wall.

Tap a tower and its range shows as a ring on the road. Pay for an upgrade or one of its abilities before the next creep rounds the bend.

The road changes as you go: a green meadow, a forest at dusk, snow, and a volcano of ash and dead white trees. A werewolf crosses a wooden bridge. A dragon breathes fire over the gate.

We'll put the App Store and Google Play links here when it's out.

Leave No Witnesses

We're still making this one. It's a first-person stealth-action game for PC: get in, find your target, get out. Guards hunt you by sight and sound, civilians run for help, and anything you leave behind can give you away.

Pools of darkness hide you and footsteps carry, so crouch behind the bar and let the guard walk past.

Mistakes stay where you left them. A wounded guard bleeds and screams, blood trails get followed, and bodies left in the open get found.

Screenshots are from the current build. There's no Steam page yet.
