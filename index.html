Leave No Witnesses

We're still making this one. It's a first-person stealth-action game for PC: get in, find your target, get out. Guards hunt you by sight and sound, civilians run for help, and anything you leave behind can give you away.

Pools of darkness hide you and footsteps carry, so crouch behind the bar and let the guard walk past.

Mistakes stay where you left them. A wounded guard bleeds and screams, blood trails get followed, and bodies left in the open get found.
